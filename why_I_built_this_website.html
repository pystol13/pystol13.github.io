Why do this when Squarespace is easier?

Because I wanted to show myself I could. There are so many other ways to set up a website quickly. It makes no economic sense to build your own website anymore, just use one of the many tools that are available. It will be so much faster. It will look nicer too. It will fit in what the casual viewer sees as "normal," or "legitimate."

I built this to satisfy my own curiousity. Because it will look the exact way I want it to. Because I have found an there tends to be an inverse correlation between design and utility. I like things that do what they say they do. Also, I miss the early internet websites, if only for their style.

Why build a boat by hand? There are master craftsmen who will do it faster and better. Why build a garden? You can buy vegetables at the store.

Why cook? It makes more economic sense to outsource soutsource outsource. You should focus solely on the key lever that matters - your work, your roduct, your role, your job. Maximize top line. In truth, there is validity to being good, even great! at one's work. But do so only when cognizant of the cost. Just look at our country, our neighborhoods, when we "optimize" for revenue. What happens to our culture?

Why build a family? You can outsource most family related tasks to others, be it school, sports teams, TV. You can hire a nanny to feed and bathe your child. You can even hire a surrogate to carry the child to term. You can focus on tasks with higher economic returns. Why aren't you? Why do you insist on cooking for your family?

These ideas, taken to their logical end, create ants. Ants, cogs, widgets in a system that creates... what? Are we better off? Are you better off being a successful ant than you are being a so-so human?

Try to build your own rowboat. Build your own garden. Insist on being the primary imprint on your child. Take joy in creating the things you interact with.

And then build your own website too.
